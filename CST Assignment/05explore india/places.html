Famous Places in India

1. Taj Mahal

An iconic ivory-white marble mausoleum on the right bank of the Yamuna river in Agra.

2. Qutub Minar

A UNESCO World Heritage Site in Delhi, standing as one of the tallest brick minarets in the world.

3. Gateway of India

An arch monument built during the 20th century in the city of Mumbai, facing the Arabian Sea.

4. Hawa Mahal

The "Palace of Breeze" in Jaipur, constructed from red and pink sandstone with intricate lattice work.

5. Baga Beach

A famous coastal spot in Goa known for its golden sands, nightlife, and water sports.
